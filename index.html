What if: our own final

At most 75% of the round before the final can qualify; a bigger number is cut down to 75%. People who were in the real final keep their final result; extra qualifiers are ranked after them by their place in the previous round. One-round events are unchanged. While this is on, “Who gets points” is ignored.

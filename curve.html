Points curve

How points are given out in each event of a Swisscubing Cup competition.

In every event, competitors are ranked and given points by place. There are two kinds of points systems to choose from on the standings page: curves, which go from 100 for 1st to 0 for last and stretch to fit the number of competitors, and fixed tables, which give the same points for a place however many people competed.

Steep at the top

points = 100 × (1 − ln r / ln N)

Winning matters most: the gap between 1st and 2nd is the biggest, and each place further down loses fewer points than the one before. The lower places end up close together.

Gentle at the top

points = 100 × ln(N + 1 − r) / ln N

The mirror image of the steep curve. The top places are close together and the points only drop quickly near the bottom of the ranking.

Linear + podium bonus

points = 100 × (N − r) / (N − 1) + bonus

Points go down by the same amount for every place, from 100 for 1st to 0 for last. On top of that the podium gets a bonus: +20 for 1st, +10 for 2nd and +5 for 3rd, so a win is worth 120 points. Tied competitors get the bonus of the place they share.

Fixed tables

Each place gets a set number of points, whatever the number of competitors. Places past 50th get 0. Set N to 51 or more below to see every value.

  • Thomas option 1 (gaps every 10 places): 250, 220, 200, 180, then 5 points less per place down to 150 for 10th. After every 10th place there is a bigger drop (150 → 130 for 11th, 94 → 74 for 21st, 47 → 37 for 31st, 19 → 10 for 41st), and in between the steps shrink from 4 to 3 to 2 to 1 point per place, ending at 1 for 50th.
  • Thomas option 2 (no gaps): the same top 10 (250 down to 150), then steady steps without the bigger drops: 6 points per place to 90 for 20th, 5 per place to 40 for 30th, 2 per place to 18 for 41st, then 9 for 42nd and 1 point less per place down to 1 for 50th.
  • Daniel: 200, 170, 150, 135, then 6 points less per place to 87 for 12th, 4 per place to 59 for 19th, about 3 per place to 39 for 25th, 2 per place to 13 for 38th, then 1 per place to 1 for 50th.

What r and N are

  • r is the competitor's place (1 = best).
  • N is the number of ranked competitors in the event. With “Only finalists” that is the people in the final round; with “Everyone in the event” it is everyone who competed in the event.
  • Competitors without a valid result (DNF/DNS) get 0 and are not counted in N.
  • Tied competitors share the same place and get the same points.
  • If only one person is ranked, they get 100 on a curve (120 with the podium bonus) or 1st-place points on a fixed table.
  • N only changes the curves. The fixed tables ignore it.

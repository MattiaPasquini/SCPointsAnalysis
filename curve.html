Points curve

How points are given out in each event of a Swisscubing Cup competition.

In every event, competitors are ranked and given points between 100 (1st place) and 0 (last place). The places in between follow a curve. There are three curves to choose from on the standings page:

Steep at the top

points = 100 × (1 − ln r / ln N)

Winning matters most: the gap between 1st and 2nd is the biggest, and each place further down loses fewer points than the one before. The lower places end up close together.

Gentle at the top

points = 100 × ln(N + 1 − r) / ln N

The mirror image of the steep curve. The top places are close together and the points only drop quickly near the bottom of the ranking.

Linear + podium bonus

points = 100 × (N − r) / (N − 1) + bonus

Points go down by the same amount for every place, from 100 for 1st to 0 for last. On top of that the podium gets a bonus: +20 for 1st, +10 for 2nd and +5 for 3rd, so a win is worth 120 points. Tied competitors get the bonus of the place they share.

What r and N are

  • r is the competitor's place (1 = best).
  • N is the number of ranked competitors in the event. With “Only finalists” that is the people in the final round; with “Everyone in the event” it is everyone who competed in the event.
  • Competitors without a valid result (DNF/DNS) get 0 and are not counted in N.
  • Tied competitors share the same place and get the same points.
  • If only one person is ranked, they get 100 (120 with the podium bonus).
